# WRONGSIDE S01E01 — The Day the Floor Quit
Target duration: 15:00. Standalone pilot.

## Core conflict
On the morning of the Pancake Parade, Wrongside's living sidewalks go on strike after years of being mistreated. Pip tries a machine; Ziggy investigates; Bongo notices the workers' perspective. The town cleans up and the sidewalks return. A rolling giant pancake threatens the syrup fountain; the characters use the sidewalk's cooperation to avert disaster.
