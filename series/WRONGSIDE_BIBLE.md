# WRONGSIDE — GOOBTV Original Series
## Series identity
15-minute standalone 3D animated absurdist comedy for roughly ages 7–12. Each episode has a coherent problem, escalating consequences, and a resolution. No random visual gag can contradict the central problem's rules.

## World rules
- Wrongside is a small, crooked, colorful town. Mundane objects may be alive, but they have specific capabilities and limitations.
- The sidewalks can slide, tilt, form signs through cracks, and carry people; they cannot speak or teleport.
- Talking objects have feelings and motives, not unlimited magic powers.
- Problems are resolved by character choices, not unexplained inventions or resets.

## Recurring cast
**Pip** — Small turquoise raccoon inventor in yellow vest, orange tool belt. Confident, fast-talking, impulsive, caring. "I have a plan. Mostly." Never cruel.
**Bongo** — Huge round purple creature, floppy ears, orange sneakers. Gentle, literal, observant; surprising solutions. "Was that supposed to happen?"
**Ziggy** — Animated yellow triangular warning sign, pink boots, expressive eyebrows. Bold, practical, knows town rules. "That's definitely against the rules."
**Mr. Drizzle** — Tiny grumpy floating gray cloud, tie, mustache, inspector hat. Rule-obsessed but redeemable. "I'm writing that down."

## Visual continuity
16:9 landscape, 1920x1080 final target, 24 fps. Soft 3D shapes, warm sunny palette, readable silhouettes, energetic physical comedy. Keep the characters' exact colors, clothes, proportions, and voices consistent. No text-only slideshow substitutes for completed animation.

## Voice and audio
English only. Pip: quick mid-high voice. Ziggy: confident bright voice. Bongo: deep relaxed voice. Drizzle: crisp officious nasal voice. Mouth animation must be checked on closeups. Use original or licensed music and effects.

## Story engine
1. Normal routine interrupted by one absurd rule.
2. Pip rushes to solve the symptom.
3. Ziggy uncovers the real cause.
4. Bongo makes an unexpected useful observation.
5. Their teamwork resolves the issue.
6. End tag adds a joke, not a cliffhanger requirement.

## Production gates
1. Approve character turnaround/model sheets.
2. Build reusable rigged 3D models and town set.
3. Render 30–75 second proof-of-concept scene with spoken English and physical motion.
4. Approve visual and audio quality before generating full 15-minute episode.
5. Produce episode in separately rendered scenes and edit together.
**Current repository Blender prototype does not satisfy gates 1–3.**
